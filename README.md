# appledhd

A hand-drawn personal planner — calendar, tasks, hour-tracked goals, menstrual
cycle, budget and a notepad, all over your own artwork.

Everything lives on your own device. No account, no server, nothing sent
anywhere — your planner is yours.

**Try it:** [appledhd.vercel.app](https://appledhd.vercel.app/)

## What's in it

- **Calendar & tasks** — plan your day, colour-coded by how urgent things are
- **Goals** — track hours toward anything you're chipping away at
- **Cycle tracking** — log periods, see predictions
- **Budget** — a running tally of what's left to spend
- **Notepad** — checklists and sticky-note boards
- A small daily streak, a guided first-run tour, and a transparency setting so
  your artwork shows through as much or as little as you like

## Built with

Next.js, wrapped in Capacitor to ship as an Android and iOS app alongside the
web version.
